ABOUT ME


the name "kotka-la" comes from a cute wordplay
mixing Pomak word for "cat" (kotka)
and Turkish suffix for "-ify (it)" (-la).

my artistic journey began with ceramics
and evolved into creating original embroidery pieces.

with a background in art education,
i bring my own artistic perspective
to traditional embroidery,
using thread as a way to tell stories.

i'm deeply inspired by women's craftsmanship
and the natural world.
animal motifs, vibrant colors and the beauty of
handmade work are at the heart of everything i create.

therefore the brand's name reflects this vibe
and the joyful energy very good.


let's catify it together! ♡
